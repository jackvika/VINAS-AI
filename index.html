3 Get your text

With the AI writer on, your text goes to this site's own server and from there to Cloudflare Workers AI, which writes the new version. Cloudflare says it does not train on this text or keep it. The word brain runs on this site's server. This site does not save or share your text. With both off, it stays in your browser. Do not paste confidential text. AI can make mistakes, so compare the result with your original. This site does not try to get past AI detectors.
