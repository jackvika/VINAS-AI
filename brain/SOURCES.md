# What the brain learned from

`human.bin` holds counts of word groups (up to four words), not the texts. It was made with `train.py` on 24 November 2026 from:

- **Open American National Corpus (OANC)**, <https://anc.org/data/oanc/>, by the American National Corpus Consortium: speech, magazine articles, letters, travel guides, government reports and other American English. Biomedical papers were left out. The corpus site calls it unrestricted in use and redistribution; the `license.txt` in the download sets conditions on passing on the texts themselves. Read it before you share the texts.
- **Tatoeba**, <https://tatoeba.org>, English sentences with numbers below 11,000,000, under [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/), by the Tatoeba contributors.

Neither project endorses this site.
